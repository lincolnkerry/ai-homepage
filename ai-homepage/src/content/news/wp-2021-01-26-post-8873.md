On January 20, Professor Heung-No Lee was appointed as the Director of the GIST AI Research Institute.
The Professor is committed to expanding GIST's role in advancing the nations' efforts in Artificial Intelligence.
The main focus of the Professor's vision is fostering startups working in AI+Finance and AI+Blockchain areas.
