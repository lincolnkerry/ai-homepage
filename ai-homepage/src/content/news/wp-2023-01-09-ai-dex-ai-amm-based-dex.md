AI-DEX introduces an innovative approach in decentralized exchange (DEX) technology, utilizing Automated Market Maker (AMM) algorithms and Price Oracle technology. The key aspect of AI-DEX is its ability to mitigate impermanent loss, a significant financial risk for liquidity providers (LPs) in AMM-based DEXs. By integrating AI-driven price prediction with on-chain smart contracts, AI-DEX accurately forecasts market prices, reducing market value errors, slippage, and price impact issues. This is achieved through comprehensive market trade data analysis and dynamic curve control techniques, enhancing user experience and addressing liquidity challenges in DEX.
