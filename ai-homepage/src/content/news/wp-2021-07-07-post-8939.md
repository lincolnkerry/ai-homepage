INFONET laboratory (GIST, South Korea) currently seeking excellent and highly motivated Scientists with a PhD degree (for Postdoc position) and MS degree (for researcher position) in computer science to join our team and develop algorithms and applications related to Blockchain and Machine learning (starting date: August 2021). Details of our laboratory research projects are available at our lab website <https://cfq.ckd.mybluehost.me/heungno2/?page_id=56>. The selected candidate will work under the supervision of Prof. Heung-No Lee.
**Research Topics:** Blockchain and Machine learning for various applications.
**Qualification:** The candidate must have obtained MS degree in Computer Science with good grades for researcher position and Ph.D. degree in Electrical/Computer Science Engineering for Post-Doctoral positions. Persons with excellent programming skills are encouraged to apply.
**Benefits:** Housing inside campus, salary range from 33,000,000~50,000,000 KRW/year for researcher position and 40,000,000~70,000,000 KRW/year for post-doctoral position based on experience.
**About us:** INFONET (<https://cfq.ckd.mybluehost.me/heungno2/>) is a research lab under the School of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science (<https://eecs.gist.ac.kr/eecs/>) in Gwangju Institute of Science and Technology (GIST) (<https://www.gist.ac.kr/en/main.html>). GIST established on Nov.17, 1993, is one of the top public universities in Gwangju, South Korea. It is ranked 9th in South Korea, 4th in the world number of citations per faculty, and 295 in the world; QS Global World Rankings 2021. Ranked 3rd in South Korea and 38th in the world university ranking for schools with a history of less than 50 years; QS Global World Rankings.
**We offer:**

* A creative environment for research.
* A work environment that is well-equipped with the newest hardware and software technology.
* The possibility to present your research results at international top conferences.

**We expect:**

* Good scientific communication and writing skills
* Ability to do original research and verify the research finding via computer simulation (Python, C++, Java)
* Ability to disseminate research results to top conferences and journals

**How to apply:** Please send your CV, cover letter, one-page research statement (only for postdoc candidates), and links to any open-source contributions to the following E-mail IDs: [heungno@gist.ac.kr](mailto:heungno@gist.ac.kr) and [yaseen@gm.gist.ac.kr](mailto:yaseen@gm.gist.ac.kr) with the title “GIST prospective Researcher/Postdoc candidate”.
**Interview process:**
Phase 1: Document Screening.
Phase 2 interview: The shortlisted candidate will be contacted by the supervisor for an online interview.
For further information, please contact Prof. Heung-No Lee ([heungno@gist.ac.kr](mailto:heungno@gist.ac.kr)) and see <https://cfq.ckd.mybluehost.me/heungno2/>.
**Deadline for applications:** Until position filled.
