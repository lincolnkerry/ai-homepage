##### My research

My research focuses on EEG (Electroencephalography) based brain-computer interface (BCI). Specifically, EEG signal processing using pattern recognition and machine learning techniques is main research interest. My research contributions are to design efficient EEG pattern recognition schemes for real-time BCI systems. I propose a “sparse representation based classification scheme” by using idea of sparse representation and L1 minimization tool and it shows robust classification performance in EEG classification.
