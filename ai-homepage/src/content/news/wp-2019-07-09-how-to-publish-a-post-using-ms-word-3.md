When publishing a blog for the first time

1. Open word document that you wish to publish as blog post
2. In the word document navigate to File > Share > Post to Blog then click a button "Post to Blog"

   ![](https://cfq.ckd.mybluehost.me/heungno2/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/070919_0206_Howtopublis1.png)
3. Enter post Title in the newly created blog-version of your document

   ![](https://cfq.ckd.mybluehost.me/heungno2/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/070919_0206_Howtopublis2.png)
4. Then in panel chose Blog Post > Publish

   ![](https://cfq.ckd.mybluehost.me/heungno2/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/070919_0206_Howtopublis3.png)
5. In the dialog window "Register a Blog Account", press "Register an Account"

   ![](https://cfq.ckd.mybluehost.me/heungno2/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/070919_0206_Howtopublis4.png)
6. Then in drop down menu chose "Wordpress" as your blog and press next

   ![](https://cfq.ckd.mybluehost.me/heungno2/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/070919_0206_Howtopublis5.png)
7. Enter your blogpost url <https://cfq.ckd.mybluehost.me/heungno2/xmlrpc.php>

   ![](https://cfq.ckd.mybluehost.me/heungno2/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/070919_0206_Howtopublis6.png)
8. Register your blog account by entering your www.infonet.gist.ac.kr credentials (tick remember password for future posts)

   ![](https://cfq.ckd.mybluehost.me/heungno2/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/070919_0206_Howtopublis7.png)
9. In the dialog window "Connect to INFONET", enter your user name and password (tick remember password for future posts)

Publishing after registration

1. Simply press Blog Post > Publish in the panel if you saved your password in the previous step 9)
