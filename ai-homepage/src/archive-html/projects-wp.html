Research Portfolio

INFONET Lab conducts research on trustworthy intelligent systems that sense, process, verify, and connect information. Our current projects connect Web3 trust infrastructure, zero-knowledge sensing, cryptographic authentication, blockchain cloud services, decentralized consensus, RF fingerprinting, hyperspectral imaging, and AI-based signal recovery.

  • AI for knowledge-intensive and adaptive systems: RAG, agentic AI, continual learning, knowledge-grounded reasoning, autonomous research assistants, and AI systems that continuously retrieve, learn, reason, and make decisions in dynamic environments.
  • Hyperspectral AI and intelligent sensing systems: HSI-based perception, computational spectroscopy, spectral reconstruction, multimodal scientific sensing, AI-based classification, and high-dimensional signal analysis for scientific and industrial intelligence.
  • Zero-knowledge sensing and security: methods for verifying sensing results and protecting information without exposing sensitive data.
  • Web3 and blockchain trust infrastructure: decentralized consensus, verifiable computation, cryptographic authentication, and blockchain-based cloud services.
