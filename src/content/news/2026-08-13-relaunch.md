INFONET is rebuilding its homepage around a simple principle: **humans provide raw
material — Slack discussions, slides, papers, commits — and AI agents turn it into
published content**: articles, audio summaries, videos, and shorts.

The new site is generated from a public GitHub repository. Every article, including
this one, arrives as a pull request: an AI journalist drafts it, a human approves the
merge, and the site redeploys automatically.

What to expect here:

- **News** — research stories mined from the lab's daily discussions
- **Publications** — 240+ papers, searchable, each linked to code and demos
- **Biz** — WorldLand, LiberVance, 지름길 Shortcut, and GIST BizRouter, ready to try
- **An AI concierge** — ask anything about our research and get an instant answer

Full relaunch: **August 20, 2026**.
