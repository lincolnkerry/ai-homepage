Hello world!

My research team and I will be in New York during the coming week starting from May 12th to May 16th attending [NY Consensus 2019](https://www.coindesk.com/events/). Our booth space is shown in the attached jpeg files. We will be sharing our booth space with the Crypto Valley Association from Zug, Swiss. I will be also attending SEC night, in which crypto mom Hester Peirce and other regulators will address current US regulations on cryptos during a dinner networking event Monday the 13th. I will also be present at Skyroom Tuesday evening 14th and join the panel discussion. During the CVA booth hours, I will talk about our research project development and deployment schedule of DeSecure blockchains.

NY Consensus is one of the most well-known cryptocurrency and blockchain conference. This year it will be held from 13th May to 15th May. The venue is at the New York Hilton Midtown Hotel. Our team will be staying at this hotel as well.

The following tables are to let you know our schedule when we will be present in the booth space 338/340 from 13th to 15th May. Anyone who wants to meet us in New York, please do not hesitate to call on us!.
