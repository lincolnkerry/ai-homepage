This course focuses on the integration of blockchain and generative AI, exploring how these two technologies can interact and complement each other. In the blockchain segment, it delves deeply into the technical aspects and philosophical foundations of cryptocurrencies like Bitcoin and Ethereum, offering insights into the distribution of wealth and the future of financial systems. The generative AI section examines Transformer-based models like GPT, LLaMA, and DeepSeek, exploring the advancements in AI and methods to enhance productivity. The course aims to explore new technological and economic possibilities through the convergence of these two innovative technologies, providing students with forward-thinking skills and problem-solving abilities.
